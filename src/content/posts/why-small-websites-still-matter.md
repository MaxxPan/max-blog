Small websites are useful because the whole system can still fit in your head.

You can understand the content model, route structure, build pipeline, styling choices, and deployment path without turning the work into a platform. That makes a personal blog a good place to practice fundamentals.

The constraints are healthy: fast pages, clear navigation, durable content, and enough design to make reading comfortable.
